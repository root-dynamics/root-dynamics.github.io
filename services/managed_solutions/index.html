Every business has a different starting point. Here are a few things you might be wondering.

Can you work alongside our internal IT team?

Yes. Support can focus on the areas where your team needs help. We’ll discuss responsibilities and the right scope before getting started.

Do you provide around-the-clock monitoring?

We offer 24/7 infrastructure monitoring. Monitoring coverage, support availability, and response expectations are defined as part of your service agreement.

Can we start with a specific issue?

Yes. A recurring problem or an area that needs attention is a useful starting point. We can assess it together before deciding on ongoing support.
